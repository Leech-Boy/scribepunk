A bolt of human will streaks across the continent; bound by duty, bound by love, bound for glory, we triumph.

Unphased by obstacles imposed by a broken system; using our own might, drive till my butt hurts, burning the black blood, of the Earth.

The ease that was promised is replaced with toil, no food, no drink, no sleep, yet.

If they thought about us at all they would pity us. We don't need them. We don't love them. We will treat them tit for tat.

Electricity in the air draws us inexorably towards our goal; rock on the fm, caff in my bloodstream, pain in my body, keeps me awake.
